The people behind the platform

Better supply. Better building.

BUILTASUPPLY is a practical procurement marketplace for construction teams. We make it easier to discover dependable supplies, order with clarity, and keep every project moving.

Why we exist

Procurement should feel like progress.

Every build has enough moving parts. Finding the right materials shouldn’t add more friction. BUILTASUPPLY brings product discovery, supplier relationships, and order visibility into one clear experience designed for the realities of project work.

We believe good tools should stay out of the way: easy to understand, dependable under pressure, and built around people who need to get things done.
